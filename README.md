### Diversity and Inclusion Dashboard Using Power BI

#### Introduction
The Diversity and Inclusion Dashboard, created using Power BI, is designed to provide insights into the diversity and inclusion metrics within an organization. It focuses on job levels, performance ratings, and promotion statistics, segmented by gender and other categories. This dashboard helps organizations understand the distribution of male and female employees across different job levels, their performance ratings, and the impact of promotions on these metrics.

### Key Sections and Visualizations

#### Filters
- **Department**: Options include Finance, HR, Internal Services, Operations, Sales & Marketing, and Strategy.
- **Job Level**: Ranges from 1 - Executive to 6 - Junior Officer.
- **Region Group**: Options include Elsewhere, Europe, and Switzerland.
- **Age Group**: A dropdown menu to select different age groups.
- **Gender Distribution**: Displays the percentage of females (41%) and males (59%).

#### Visualizations
1. **Job Level after FY20 promotions**
   - **Type**: Horizontal Bar Chart
   - **Description**: Shows the percentage of females and males at different job levels after FY20 promotions.

2. **FY19 Performance Rating of Male after FY20 promotions**
   - **Type**: Line Chart
   - **Description**: Shows the performance ratings of males who left or stayed after FY20 promotions across different job levels.

3. **FY19 Performance Rating of Female after FY20 promotions**
   - **Type**: Line Chart
   - **Description**: Shows the performance ratings of females who left or stayed after FY20 promotions across different job levels.

4. **Job Level after FY21 promotions**
   - **Type**: Bar Chart with Line Overlay
   - **Description**: Shows the number of females and males at different job levels after FY21 promotions, along with the percentage of females.

5. **Sum of Time in Job Level after FY21 promotions**
   - **Type**: Horizontal Bar Chart
   - **Description**: Shows the sum of time spent by females and males at different job levels after FY21 promotions.

### Questions Asked
- What is the gender distribution across different job levels after FY20 and FY21 promotions?
- How do performance ratings vary for males and females who left or stayed after FY20 promotions?
- What is the sum of time spent by employees at different job levels after FY21 promotions?

### KPIs Covered
1. **Gender Distribution Percentages**: The percentage of females and males in the organization.
2. **Job Level Distribution after Promotions**: The distribution of employees by job level after FY20 and FY21 promotions.
3. **Performance Ratings by Gender and Job Level**: Performance ratings of employees categorized by gender and job level.
4. **Sum of Time Spent in Job Levels by Gender**: The total time employees have spent at different job levels, categorized by gender.

### Insights
1. **Job Level Distribution After FY20 Promotions**:
   - Executive: 87.50% male, 12.50% female
   - Director: 86.49% male, 13.51% female
   - Senior Manager: 82.14% male, 17.86% female
   - Manager: 63.41% male, 36.59% female
   - Senior Officer: 53.33% male, 46.67% female
   - Junior Officer: 46.57% male, 53.43% female

2. **Job Level Distribution After FY21 Promotions**:
   - Executive: 16% female, 3% male
   - Director: 19% female, 4% male
   - Senior Manager: 25% female, 11% male
   - Manager: 50% female, 24% male
   - Senior Officer: 46% female, 54% male
   - Junior Officer: 78% female, 91% male

3. **Performance Ratings After FY20 Promotions**:
   - Male: Performance ratings vary across job levels, with a notable difference between those who left the organization and those who stayed.
   - Female: Performance ratings also vary, with a similar trend observed.

4. **Sum of Time in Job Level After FY21 Promotions**:
   - Executive: 12 female, 38 male
   - Director: 10 female, 84 male
   - Senior Manager: 24 female, 107 male
   - Manager: 57 female, 92 male
   - Senior Officer: 112 female, 129 male
   - Junior Officer: 163 female, 179 male

### Conclusion
The Diversity and Inclusion Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of diversity and inclusion metrics within the organization. It highlights the distribution of male and female employees across different job levels, their performance ratings, and the impact of promotions. The insights gained from this dashboard can help organizations identify areas for improvement in their diversity and inclusion efforts and make data-driven decisions to promote a more inclusive workplace.
